GOT QUESTIONS?

We have transparent answers

Understanding your dietary shifts should not feel complicated.

Does SafePlate provide professional medical advice? ▼

No. SafePlate is a supportive, informative platform designed to guide your wellness journey. We work alongside dietitian standards, but we always encourage discussing your health directly with a licensed physician.

How do you verify restaurant food safety? ▼

Our verification panel analyzes menu ingredients and kitchen cross-contact avoidance processes submitted by cafe owners. Verified items receive different tiers of safety scores based on strict protocols.

Can I manage multiple allergy profiles under one account? ▼

Yes! Our family profiles allow you to bundle overlapping restrictions (e.g. low-sodium vegan + nut-free celiac) to find meals that are safe for everyone.
